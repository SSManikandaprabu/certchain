Frequently Asked Questions

Do I or my students need cryptocurrency or a crypto wallet?
No. CertChain abstracts all on-chain gas operations through simple fiat billing (UPI, cards). Students receive a standard PDF certificate with an embedded verification QR code.
What if CertChain shuts down in the future?
Your certificates remain 100% verifiable forever. Because the Merkle roots are committed immutably to Polygon's public blockchain, anyone can recompute the PDF's hash and verify it against Polygon directly using open-source tools.
Can certificates be faked or altered?
No. Even altering a single pixel or character in the PDF changes its SHA-256 hash completely, causing an instant cryptographic mismatch on Polygon.
What does verification cost for employers?
Verification is 100% free for everyone, forever. No accounts or fees required.
Is student data stored on the public blockchain?
Never. Only anonymous 32-byte cryptographic SHA-256 hashes are anchored on Polygon, ensuring full compliance with GDPR and FERPA.
